      The road to the platinum

      Work the stages in order. Story trophies come first because the missable moments live inside the story; collectibles and challenges wait until Leonida is open to you. The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, unlocks when the other 54 are earned.

        Missable trophies

        A trophy is flagged missable when its official description ties it to a one-time story moment or a window that closes as the story moves on — a partner beside you, a single set-piece, a departure that happens once. These are the ones to watch for on your first playthrough; everything else can be picked up in free roam afterwards.
